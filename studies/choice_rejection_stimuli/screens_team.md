# Choice vs. rejection frame: 100 stimuli for the twin study

Each twin sees all 100 stimuli in both frames, and every stimulus x frame is a separate call with no memory of the others. Each stimulus has three screens. Screen 1 is a scenario. Screen 2 is a lead-in plus ONE line: the choice frame (the other person chose B) or the rejection frame (the other person did not choose A). The two lines are word for word the same except for the decision. Screen 3 asks for a preference on a 7-point scale (1 = strongly prefer the option listed first, 4 = no preference, 7 = strongly prefer the other); which option is listed first is random per twin x stimulus, and screen 1 describes the options in that order. Outcome: mean preference for B, by frame.

Fixed text around each stimulus: screen 1 starts with *"Please take a moment to put yourself in the scenario below."*; screen 3 is *"Which of the two options would you prefer?"* with the 7-point scale.


## s001: consumer electronics

**Screen 1.** Imagine that you are shopping in an electronics store for a new smartphone for your mother, whose old phone has stopped working. You have narrowed it down to two models that cost about the same.

> **Large-screen phone:** The Large-screen phone has a big, bright display that makes text and photos easy to read, though it is heavier and harder to hold in one hand.
>
> **Compact phone with better camera:** The Compact phone with better camera is small and light and takes sharper photos, especially of family and in dim rooms, but its screen is smaller and the text is harder to read.

Please think about which of the two options you would prefer.

**Screen 2.** While you stand at the display table comparing the two phones, an electronics salesperson walks over and tells you:

- *Choice frame:* I had a customer yesterday choosing between the two phones for her mother, and she bought the compact one.
- *Rejection frame:* I had a customer yesterday choosing between the two phones for her mother, and she did not buy the large-screen one.

**Screen 3.** A. Large-screen phone / B. Compact phone with better camera (B = the option the other person ended up with)
