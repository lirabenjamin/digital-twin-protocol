The method, in plain sight

Maximize F − λD. F is the high-minus-low focal rating. D summarizes movement on all 19 other rated features. A larger focal gap always wins when D is equal. Each additional unit of D costs λ points of F. Negative scores simply mean the penalty exceeds the gap; compare scores only within the same moderator and λ.

  • The 100 stimuli each have 20 features, with three LLM ratings per feature. These are exploratory design ratings, not human ground truth.
  • Low must be in the bottom empirical tertile and high in the top, including boundary ties, with high strictly greater than low. There is no two-point minimum or non-focal tolerance.
  • D uses the fixed correlation geometry from all 100 stimuli, excluding the focal feature. If R = Q diag(η) Qᵀ, W = Q diag(1/max(η,1)) Qᵀ and D = √(ΔᵀWΔ). Δ contains original rating-point differences. Correlated common movement is discounted; no direction is amplified. This is a proposed distance, not standard Mahalanobis distance or proof that correlated constructs are identical.
  • Ties prefer smaller D, then larger F, then stimulus IDs. Numerical sorting keys round to ten decimal places. The plot shows all eligible pairs; no PCA projection or outcomes enter these coordinates.
  • The three comparison moderators had small twin-study point estimates; they are not established null effects. Pair searches permit stimulus reuse.
  • These targeted pairs are not a representative sample for a population mean. A single pair does not isolate a moderator from every other scenario difference.
